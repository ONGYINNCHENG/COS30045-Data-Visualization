Television Energy Usage

Televisions are widely used in Australian living rooms. The energy consumption of a TV depends heavily on screen size, display technology, and picture settings.

Typical Power Consumption by Display Type

Technology Screen Size Typical Power (Watts)
LED / LCD 43 Inches 50W - 75W
QLED 55 Inches 90W - 130W
OLED 65 Inches 120W - 180W

Energy Saving Tips for TVs

  • Enable automatic brightness sensing to adapt to ambient lighting.
  • Disable instant-on standby modes when not in use.
  • Switch off at the wall socket during extended absences.
